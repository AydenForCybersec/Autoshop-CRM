# Adding Customers and Vehicles

Every job starts with a customer. Before you can create a work order, you need the customer and their vehicle in the system. This page walks you through both.

## Step 1 — Check if the customer already exists

Before adding anyone new, check if they are already in the system. Click **Customers** in the top menu. You will see a list of everyone you have ever entered.

Scroll through or use the search box at the top of the list to look up by name. If you find them, click their name to open their profile. Skip ahead to Step 3.

## Step 2 — Add a new customer

If the customer is not in the system yet, click the **Add Customer** button on the Customers page. Fill in what you know:

- **Name** — required. First and last name.
- **Phone** — their best contact number. Just type the digits — the system formats it automatically.
- **Email** — optional.
- **Address** — optional. Useful for mailing invoices.

Click **Add Customer** when you are done. You will land on their profile page.

## Step 3 — Check if their vehicle is already on file

On the customer's profile page, scroll down. You will see a list of vehicles already attached to this customer. If the vehicle you are working on is already there, click its year/make/model to open it. Skip ahead to Step 5.

## Step 4 — Add their vehicle

Click **Add Vehicle** on the customer's profile page. Fill in:

- **Year** — the model year of the vehicle
- **Make** — the brand (Ford, Chevy, Dodge, etc.)
- **Model** — the specific model (F-150, Silverado, Ram 1500, etc.)
- **VIN** — optional, but useful for warranty tracking. It is the 17-character number usually on the dashboard near the windshield.
- **Plate** — the license plate number, optional

If a similar vehicle already exists in the system, a warning will pop up. Read it carefully. If it is the same vehicle, click **Use Existing** — do not create a duplicate. If it truly is a different vehicle, click **Add Anyway**.

Click **Add Vehicle** when you are done. You will land on the vehicle's detail page.

## Step 5 — Create a work order (job)

On the vehicle's page, scroll down to the **Add Job** section on the right side. You only need to fill in one thing:

- **Description** — a short note about what the job is. For example: `Brake pad replacement, front axle` or `Oil change and tire rotation`.

You can also fill in **Entry Date** if you are backdating an old job. Leave it blank to use today's date.

Click **Add Job**. The job will appear in the list on the left. Its status starts as **Open**.

## Step 6 — Update the job status as you work

On the vehicle page, next to each job you will see a small dropdown that says **Open**. As the job progresses, change it:

- **Open** — just created, not started yet
- **In Progress** — someone is actively working on it
- **On Hold** — waiting on parts or customer approval
- **Completed** — work is done, ready to invoice

Click **Update** after changing the status. This is how you track where every job stands.

## A typical day

Most mornings look like this:

1. Click **Customers**, find the customer whose car came in
2. Click their vehicle
3. Check if there is already an open job — if so, update its status or add parts/labor
4. If it is a new visit, add a new job with a description of today's work
5. At the end of the job, add parts and log labor hours
6. When ready, print the invoice
