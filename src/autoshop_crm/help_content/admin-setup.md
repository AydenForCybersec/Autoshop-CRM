# Owner and Admin Setup

This guide is for the shop owner or whoever is in charge of running the program. It covers setting up your shop information, configuring tax and card fees, and getting your staff set up with accounts.

## Step 1 — Enter your shop information

Click **Settings** in the top menu. You should be on the **Business** tab automatically.

Fill in:

- **Shop Name** — the name that will appear on every invoice
- **Phone** — your shop's main phone number
- **Email** — your shop's email address (optional)
- **Address** — your shop's full street address. This prints on invoices. You can type it on multiple lines — press Enter between the street address and the city/state line.
- **Upload New Logo** — if you have a logo image saved on the computer, click this to upload it. It will appear at the top of every invoice. Accepted formats: PNG, JPG, WebP.

Click **Save Business Profile** when you are done.

## Step 2 — Set your tax rate and card fee

Still on the **Business** tab, scroll down to find:

- **Sales Tax Rate (%)** — enter your local sales tax as a percentage. For example, for 6.225% enter `6.225`. This is already set to 6.225 by default.
- **Card Fee Rate (%)** — if you charge customers a fee for paying by card (to cover processing fees), enter that percentage here. For example, `3.0` for 3%. Leave blank or set to 0 if you do not charge a card fee.

Click **Save Business Profile** again to save these.

These rates apply automatically on every invoice. You do not need to enter them each time — the invoice page lets you toggle card payment on or off with a single checkbox.

## Step 3 — Set up user accounts for your staff

Click the **Users** tab at the top of the Settings page.

You will see your own account listed, and a form to create new accounts below it.

### Creating a new account

Fill in:

- **Username** — something simple, like their first name or first name plus last initial. No spaces. Example: `mike` or `sarahj`.
- **Password** — at least 8 characters. Give this to the employee privately.
- **Role** — see the roles explained below.

Click **Add User**.

### Roles explained

- **Owner** — full access to everything. Typically just one person.
- **Admin** — full access to everything. Good for a shop manager.
- **Staff** — can create customers, vehicles, and jobs, and print invoices. Cannot access accounting or settings.
- **Viewer** — can look things up but cannot change anything. Good for front desk staff who just need to check status.

When in doubt, start employees on **Staff**. You can always adjust later.

### Setting a mechanic's labor rate

On the Users tab, find the mechanic's name in the **Current Users** list. In the **Labor Rate ($/hr)** box, enter their hourly billing rate — what you charge the customer per hour of that mechanic's labor. For example: `95.00` for $95 an hour.

Click **Update User** to save it.

This rate is used automatically when labor hours are logged on a job. If you raise or lower a mechanic's rate, it only affects new entries going forward — old invoices keep the rate that was in effect at the time.

### Resetting a password

If a staff member forgets their password, find their name in the **Current Users** list, enter a new password in the **Reset Password** box, and click **Set New Password**. Tell them the new password privately.

### Deactivating an account

If someone leaves the shop and you do not want them to be able to log in anymore, find their name, uncheck the **Account active** box, and click **Update User**. Their account stays in the system (so their past work is still recorded) but they cannot sign in.

## Step 4 — Verify everything looks right

Before your first real job:

1. Print a test invoice on a dummy job and confirm your shop name, address, and phone appear correctly at the top
2. Confirm the tax rate is right
3. Have each staff member sign in with their own account and confirm they can reach the pages they need

If anything looks wrong, go back to Settings and fix it.
