# Printing an Invoice

When a job is finished and you are ready to charge the customer, you print an invoice. The invoice is a clean, professional document that shows everything — parts, labor, tax, and the total. It automatically pulls your shop name and contact information from your settings.

## How to get to the invoice

1. Click **Customers** in the top menu
2. Find the customer and click their name
3. Click the vehicle you worked on
4. Find the job in the list on the left
5. Click the **Invoice** button next to that job

The invoice will open in a new tab. You will see a preview of what it will look like when printed.

## What the invoice shows

At the top of the invoice:
- Your shop's logo, name, address, and phone number (pulled from Settings automatically)
- The customer's name, address, and phone number
- The invoice number (this is just the job number)
- The invoice date

In the middle:
- The vehicle's year, make, model, VIN, and license plate
- A table of line items, split into two sections:
  - **Parts** — every part you added to the job, with its price
  - **Labor** — every labor entry, showing the mechanic's name, hours worked, hourly rate, and total

At the bottom right:
- **Parts total** — everything in the parts section added up
- **Labor total** — everything in the labor section added up
- **Tax** — calculated automatically from your shop's tax rate
- **Card Fee** — only shows if the customer paid by card (see below)
- **Total Due** — the final amount

At the very bottom, the invoice includes the standard repair authorization terms and warranty disclaimer. These print automatically — you do not need to type them.

## Cash vs. card payment

At the top of the invoice page (above the actual invoice), you will see a checkbox that says **Paid by card**.

- If the customer paid cash, leave the box unchecked. No card fee is added.
- If the customer paid by card, check the box. The card fee percentage (set in your shop settings) will be added to the total automatically.

The fee is calculated on the taxed total. Sales tax is not applied to the card fee — just to the parts and labor.

## The PAID stamp

If the job status is set to **Completed**, a large orange **PAID** stamp will appear diagonally across the middle of the invoice. This lets you and the customer both know at a glance that the bill has been settled.

To get the stamp to appear, go back to the vehicle page and change the job status to **Completed**, then open the invoice again.

## Printing or saving as a PDF

At the top of the invoice page, click **Print / Save PDF**. Your computer's print window will open. From here you can:

- Send it to a printer to print a physical copy
- Choose "Save as PDF" (or "Print to PDF") to save a digital copy

When the print window opens, the top bar with the Print button disappears so the invoice prints cleanly without any extra buttons or menus on it.

## If the invoice is missing your shop information

The invoice pulls your shop name, address, and phone from your shop settings. If those fields are blank or wrong, go to **Settings** → **Business** tab and fill them in. Click **Save Business Profile**, then come back and refresh the invoice.

## If the numbers look wrong

The invoice calculates from the parts and labor you entered on the job. If a number is wrong:
- Go back to the vehicle page
- Find the job
- Check the parts list — make sure prices are entered correctly
- Check the labor entries — make sure hours and mechanic rates are right
- Come back and open the invoice again
