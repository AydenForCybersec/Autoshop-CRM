# Getting Started

This program is your shop's digital filing cabinet. Instead of paper work orders and sticky notes, everything lives here — customers, their vehicles, the jobs you did, parts used, labor hours, and invoices. You can look up any customer or job in seconds, and print a clean invoice whenever you need one.

This guide will walk you through your very first day using it.

## What you see when you log in

When you first open the program, you will see a sign-in page. Type your username and password, then click **Log In**. If you do not have a username yet, ask your shop owner or manager — they set those up.

After you sign in, you land on the **Dashboard**. Think of it like the front counter — it shows you a quick summary of what is going on. How many jobs are open, how much revenue has come in, and so on. You do not need to do anything on this page. It is just a snapshot.

## The menu across the top

At the top of every page you will see a row of links. These are how you move around:

- **Dashboard** — the summary page you land on after signing in
- **Customers** — your list of every customer in the system
- **Accounting** — revenue summaries and invoice reports
- **Settings** — shop name, tax rates, and user accounts (owner/admin only)
- **Help** — you are reading this right now
- **Logout** — click this when you are done for the day

## The basic flow of every job

Every job in this system follows the same path. Once you learn it, it will feel natural:

1. Find or add the **Customer**
2. Find or add their **Vehicle** on the customer's page
3. Add a **Job** (work order) on the vehicle's page
4. Add **Parts** and log **Labor** as you work
5. When the job is done, print an **Invoice**

That is the whole system. The rest of this help section explains each step in detail.

## If something looks wrong

Do not panic. Nothing you type gets sent anywhere — it stays right in your shop's computer. If you make a mistake, you can always go back and fix it. There is no "are you sure?" trap waiting for you.

If the page shows an error message in a colored bar at the top, read it — it usually tells you exactly what went wrong (like "customer name is required" or "hours must be a positive number").
