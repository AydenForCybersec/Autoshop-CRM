# Getting Started

Welcome to Autoshop CRM.

## Who this is for
- Staff and admins signing in for daily work
- New team members learning app navigation

## First steps
1. Open the CRM URL in your browser.
2. Sign in with your assigned username and password.
3. Confirm the top navigation loads correctly.
4. Open **Customers** and verify you can see records.
5. Open **Dashboard** and verify your account has the expected access.

## Your first record flow
1. Find an existing customer or create one.
2. Add a vehicle to that customer profile.
3. Create a job/work order from the vehicle page.
4. Save, then confirm the job appears in related views.

## Navigation basics
- **Dashboard**: snapshot of recent activity and totals
- **Customers**: customer profiles and linked vehicles
- **Help**: workflows and troubleshooting guides
- **Settings**: admin-only business and user configuration

## If this fails
- Login rejected: verify credentials and ask an admin to confirm your account is active.
- Setup page appears unexpectedly: deployment/setup may still be in progress.
- Missing menu items: your role permissions may not include those areas.

## Done when
- You can sign in.
- You can open Customers and Dashboard.
- You can create or view a complete customer -> vehicle -> job chain.
