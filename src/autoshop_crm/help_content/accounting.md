# Accounting and Reports

The Accounting section gives you a financial overview of your shop. You can see how much money has come in, what is still owed, and print a report to hand off to your bookkeeper or accountant.

## Getting to accounting

Click **Accounting** in the top menu. You need to have accounting permission to see this — if the link is missing, ask your shop owner.

## What you see on the accounting page

At the top is a date filter. You can set a start date and end date to look at a specific time period — for example, just last month, or the past week. Leave both dates blank to see everything.

Below the filter are three numbers:

- **Completed Revenue** — the total dollar amount of all jobs marked as Completed in the date range. This is money that has been earned.
- **Open Pipeline** — the total of all jobs that are still Open, In Progress, or On Hold. This is money that is coming but not collected yet.
- **Average Ticket** — the average dollar amount per job. Useful for spotting trends.

Below that is a list of recent transactions — each job in the date range with its date, customer, vehicle, status, and total.

## Exporting to a spreadsheet

If your bookkeeper or accountant wants a spreadsheet, click **Export CSV Ledger**. This downloads a file to your computer that can be opened in Excel or Google Sheets. It contains one row per job with all the details.

## Printing an invoice report

The invoice report is a printable summary of all invoices in a date range — similar to the printed report you are used to seeing. It shows:

- Each invoice date and number
- The parts total for that invoice
- The labor total for that invoice
- The total amount due
- The job status

At the bottom of the report, it totals up all the columns so you can see the grand totals for the period at a glance.

### How to print the invoice report

1. Set the **Start Date** and **End Date** on the accounting page
2. Click **Apply Filter** to make sure the list updates
3. Click **Print Invoice Report** — this opens the report in a new tab
4. Click **Print / Save PDF** at the top of the report

The printed report includes your shop's logo and name at the top, the date range in the title, and today's date in the corner — just like the reports you are used to.

## A note on totals

The parts and labor totals on the invoice report come from the individual parts and labor entries on each job. If a job shows $0.00 for parts and labor but has a total due, that means the total was set manually rather than built from parts and labor entries. Going forward, the system builds the total automatically from what you enter.
