# Common Problems and How to Fix Them

## I typed the wrong customer name, phone number, or address

No problem. Click **Customers** in the top menu, find the customer, and click their name. On their profile page you can edit and correct any of those fields. Make your change and save it.

## I cannot find a customer I know I entered

Check your spelling. Try searching by last name only, or just the first few letters. Customers are listed alphabetically.

If you still cannot find them, they may have been entered under a different name — for example, a business name instead of a person's name. Try a few different searches.

## A duplicate warning popped up when adding a customer or vehicle

The system found a record that looks similar to what you are entering. Read the message carefully.

- If the existing record **is** the same customer or vehicle — click **Use Existing**. This keeps your data clean.
- If it is **not** the same person or vehicle — click **Add Anyway**.

When in doubt, use the existing one. Having the same customer twice causes confusion later.

## I cannot sign in

First, make sure you are typing your username and password correctly. Passwords are case-sensitive — a capital letter matters.

If you are sure the password is right and it still will not let you in, your account may have been deactivated. Ask your shop owner or admin to check your account under **Settings → Users**.

If you forgot your password, you cannot reset it yourself. Ask your shop owner — they can set a new one for you under **Settings → Users → Reset Password**.

## The login page says my account is locked

After several wrong password attempts, the system temporarily locks the account to prevent unauthorized access. Wait about 15 minutes and try again. If you are still locked out, ask your shop owner.

## I cannot see the Accounting or Settings pages

Your user account may not have permission to view those sections. Only owners and admins have full access by default. Ask your shop owner to adjust your permissions under **Settings → Users**.

## The invoice shows $0.00 for parts and labor

This means no parts or labor entries have been added to the job yet. Go back to the vehicle page, find the job, add parts with their prices and log any labor hours. Then open the invoice again and the numbers will appear.

## The PAID stamp is not on the invoice

The orange PAID stamp only appears when the job status is set to **Completed**. Go back to the vehicle page, find the job, change the status to **Completed**, and click **Update**. Then open the invoice again.

## A mechanic is not showing up in the labor dropdown

The mechanic may not have a user account in the system, or their account may be set to inactive. Ask your shop owner to go to **Settings → Users** and check. If they need an account, the owner can create one there.

## The tax amount or card fee on the invoice looks wrong

The tax rate and card fee percentage are set under **Settings → Business Profile**. If those numbers are wrong, an owner or admin can correct them there. After saving, the invoice will use the updated rates.

## I logged the wrong number of hours for a labor entry

Labor entries cannot be edited after they are saved. Ask your shop owner or admin to delete the incorrect entry. Then add a new one with the correct hours.

## The program says "Not Found" when I click something

This usually means you clicked a link to something that no longer exists, or a page that moved. Click **Dashboard** or **Customers** in the top menu to get back on track.

## The page is loading slowly or not at all

The program runs on a computer inside your shop. If it is slow, that computer may be busy or need a restart. Wait 30 seconds and try refreshing the page (press **F5** on your keyboard). If it keeps happening, let your shop owner know.

## I need to reprint an old invoice

Go to **Customers**, find the customer, click their vehicle, and find the original job. Click the **Invoice** button. It will rebuild the invoice from all the parts and labor that were recorded at the time. Click **Print / Save PDF** to print it again.
