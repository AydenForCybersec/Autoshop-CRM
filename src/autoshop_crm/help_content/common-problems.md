# Common Problems

## I cannot sign in
- Check username and password.
- Ask your admin to confirm your account is active.
- Ask your admin to confirm your role still has login access.

## I cannot open a page
- You might not have permission.
- Contact admin and ask for the required role access.
- After permissions are updated, sign out and sign in again.

## I see duplicate prompts
- Review suggested records.
- Choose **use existing** or **merge** when records match.

## My data did not save
- Check required fields on the form.
- Retry once after page refresh.
- If still failing, capture the exact message and time, then report it.

## Totals or lists look wrong
- Confirm job status values are current.
- Refresh once and re-check.
- Ask admin to verify no records were left in incomplete state.
